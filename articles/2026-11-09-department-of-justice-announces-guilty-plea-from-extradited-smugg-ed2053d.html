Department of Justice Announces Guilty Plea from Extradited Smuggler

The Department of Justice announced that a Bangladeshi national extradited from Brazil pleaded guilty to multiple charges related to a conspiracy that illegally moved individuals through multiple countries and into the United States for financial gain.

The defendant admitted to taking part in a broad alien smuggling network that helped move individuals from and through Brazil, South America, Central America, and Mexico with the goal of entering the United States.

Court records detail that individuals paid substantial sums for the smuggling services, which involved hazardous travel methods including boat journeys, trekking across the Darien Gap, and crossing borders through rivers or fences.

The individual entered guilty pleas for one count of conspiracy to bring an alien into the country and two counts of bringing an alien into the country for commercial advantage and financial gain, while also agreeing to forfeit collected funds.
