Justice Department Resolves Zoning Claims With New York Village

The Department of Justice reached a settlement with the Village of Atlantic Beach to address claims that local zoning regulations subjected religious land uses to stricter standards than secular gathering places, violating federal law.

The federal agency settled claims regarding municipal zoning rules that required certain religious and educational assembly locations to secure a special exception permit while exempting nonreligious assembly spaces.

Under the terms of the resolution, the municipality agreed to modify its local code to eliminate the permit requirement for places of worship, allowing religious activities as a permitted use in a specific residential district.

The settlement terms further obligate the village to conduct staff and official training regarding federal religious land use requirements and to implement a formal process for handling related complaints.
