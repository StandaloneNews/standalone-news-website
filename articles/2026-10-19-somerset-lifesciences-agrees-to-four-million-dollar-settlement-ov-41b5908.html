Somerset Lifesciences Agrees to Four Million Dollar Settlement Over Medicare Claims

Somerset Lifesciences Inc. reached an agreement with the Department of Justice to pay four million dollars to resolve claims involving Medicare Part D payments for its generic drug Olopatadine after a brand name equivalent transitioned to over-the-counter status.

The Department of Justice announced that Somerset Lifesciences Inc. agreed to a financial resolution after allegations that the company caused Medicare Part D to pay for a generic prescription drug that was no longer eligible for coverage.

The agency stated that following a brand name version of the drug switching to over-the-counter availability, the company failed to update its own product label accordingly and continued marketing it under prescription-only labeling.

According to the Department of Justice, federal healthcare programs do not cover products that are available for retail purchase without a prescription from customers.
