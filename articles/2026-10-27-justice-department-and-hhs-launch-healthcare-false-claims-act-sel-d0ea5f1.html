Justice Department and HHS Launch Healthcare False Claims Act Self-Disclosure Pilot Program

The Department of Justice Civil Division and the Department of Health and Human Services announced a joint pilot program allowing healthcare companies to self-disclose potential False Claims Act violations, aiming to streamline resolutions and focus enforcement resources.

The initiative provides a structured framework for healthcare providers to report past civil infractions, implement corrective measures, and repay funds with a specified financial multiplier in exchange for coordinated liability releases.

Entities participating in the initiative are required to provide comprehensive documentation regarding the scope of the behavior, outline corrective steps implemented, and submit electronic disclosures to a designated federal email address.

Federal officials stated that the framework is intended to encourage corporate accountability by offering predictable incentives while allowing authorities to direct investigative capacity toward uncooperative entities.
