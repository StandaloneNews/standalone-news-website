The Department of Justice announced that a dual national residing in Florida was extradited from Germany and appeared in a Miami federal court to face accusations related to a mailing fraud operation.

According to the department, the operation involved sending deceptive letters that claimed recipients were entitled to a financial windfall, requiring them to submit payments to access the money.

Prosecutors state that funds collected from thousands of individuals, including vulnerable and elderly persons, were deposited into domestic bank accounts and subsequently transferred to accounts in Europe.
