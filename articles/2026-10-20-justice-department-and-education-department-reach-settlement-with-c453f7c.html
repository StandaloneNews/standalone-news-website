Justice Department and Education Department Reach Settlement With UNC to Resolve Discrimination Investigations

The U.S. Department of Justice and the Department of Education announced a voluntary settlement agreement with the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill to address concerns regarding antisemitism and race discrimination under federal civil rights laws.

The Department of Justice and the Department of Education reached a voluntary settlement agreement with the university to resolve investigations conducted under Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964.

Under the agreement, the university agreed to implement measures concerning antisemitism, including establishing a Title VI Coordinator position and maintaining policies to prohibit, prevent, and discipline unlawful antisemitism.

The university agreed to adopt and maintain a color-blind admissions system that refrains from using race, analyzing racial data, or altering its admissions process to change the racial composition of its student body.

The agreement includes monitoring and reporting requirements, such as the selection of an administrator to oversee implementation and the submission of quarterly reports on discrimination complaints to federal authorities.
