Department of Justice Awards Funding to Support Law Enforcement Hiring

The Department of Justice announced grant funding distributed through its COPS Hiring Program to support the hiring of law enforcement officers across state, local, tribal, and territorial agencies.

The Department of Justice announced grant awards totaling nearly $150 million through a competitive program designed to assist agencies with officer salaries and benefits.

Grant prioritization focused on several specific areas, including violent crime, school-based policing, border security, nuisance abatement, and enforcement related to squatting and encampments.

Agency officials stated that hundreds of applications were submitted, resulting in funding being distributed to nearly half of the requesting organizations.
