Defense Contractors ILC Dover and ILC Astrospace Settle Cybersecurity Fraud Allegations for $13 Million

The Department of Justice announced a $13 million settlement with ILC Dover and ILC Astrospace to resolve allegations that the companies failed to meet mandatory cybersecurity standards in federal contracts with the Army, the Missile Defense Agency, and NASA.

The companies allegedly failed to maintain required system security plans for their information systems, which are necessary to document security boundaries and implementation strategies for government contracts.

The settlement addresses claims that the contractors did not fully implement specific cybersecurity controls, potentially leaving sensitive defense information vulnerable to exploitation.

A former employee who acted as a whistleblower will receive a portion of the settlement funds for reporting the alleged cybersecurity failures under the False Claims Act.
