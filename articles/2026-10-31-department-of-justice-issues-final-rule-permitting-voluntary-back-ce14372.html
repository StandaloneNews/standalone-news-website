Department of Justice Issues Final Rule Permitting Voluntary Background Checks for Firearms Dealer Employees

The Department of Justice announced a final rule that permits Federal Firearms Licensees to voluntarily utilize the FBI background check system for certain current and prospective workers. Participation is optional and requires written employee consent.

Federal Firearms Licensees are now authorized to voluntarily use the FBI system to screen eligible current and prospective workers, a process that previously was restricted exclusively to proposed firearm transfers.

Eligible individuals include specific staff such as firearm handlers, certain manufacturing employees, and applicable independent contractors, while individuals already classified as responsible persons are excluded because they undergo separate screening.

Participating dealers will receive a transaction number and one of three designated outcomes without accessing the underlying record details, and the checks are conducted without any associated fees from the FBI.
