Contractor Admits Guilt in Kickback Scheme Involving Postal Service Repairs

The Department of Justice announced that an Oregon contractor pleaded guilty to participating in a conspiracy involving more than $465,000 in illegal kickbacks related to repair work on federal postal facilities.

The defendant admitted to paying over $465,000 in kickbacks to an employee of a prime contractor in exchange for receiving additional project assignments.

According to court filings, the individuals involved intentionally raised estimates and invoices to cover the cost of the unlawful payments.
