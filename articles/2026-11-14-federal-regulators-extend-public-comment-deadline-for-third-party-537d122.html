Federal Regulators Extend Public Comment Deadline for Third-Party Risk Guidance

Several financial regulatory agencies announced an extension for the public comment period regarding proposed guidance on managing risks related to third-party relationships, moving the deadline to mid-December.

Four financial regulatory authorities stated they will give the public additional time to review and comment on proposed guidance regarding the management of risks stemming from third-party relationships.

The Federal Reserve Board additionally announced a separate extension for the comment period concerning a proposed risk management guide tailored for traditional community banks under its supervision.

Regulatory bodies noted that the proposed text draws upon supervisory experience and insights gained from reviewing how financial institutions handle third-party risk management.
