Federal Reserve Approves Isabella Bank Corporation Acquisition and Merger Application

The Federal Reserve Board approved an application by Isabella Bank Corporation to acquire Grand River Commerce, Inc. and its subsidiary. The approval also covers the subsequent bank merger and branch operations.

The Federal Reserve Board announced it approved the application submitted by Isabella Bank Corporation located in Mount Pleasant, Michigan, to acquire Grand River Commerce, Inc. and its subsidiary, Grand River Bank, both based in Grandville, Michigan.

In addition to the parent company acquisition, the regulatory agency granted approval for Isabella Bank to merge directly with Grand River Bank.

The regulatory approval allows the acquiring institution to establish and operate new branches at the existing locations previously operated by Grand River Bank.
