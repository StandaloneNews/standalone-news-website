Department of Justice Refers Asphalt Industry Merger Investigations to State Attorneys General

The Department of Justice Antitrust Division announced the successful referral of two separate asphalt industry merger investigations to state attorneys general, leading to coordinated settlements and facility divestitures in multiple states.

The Antitrust Division examined the proposed acquisition of Suit-Kote Corporation by Colas Inc., identifying specific geographic areas in New York and Pennsylvania where the transaction would reduce asphalt supplier options, and referred the case to those states.

Following the referral regarding the Colas and Suit-Kote transaction, the participating companies and state authorities reached a settlement requiring the divestiture of four facilities located across New York and Pennsylvania.

The agency also examined a proposed acquisition involving CRH plc and Arcosa Inc., narrowing potential competition concerns to a specific local hot-mix asphalt market in New Jersey before referring the matter to the state attorney general.

Subsequent to the referral regarding the CRH and Arcosa acquisition, the involved parties reached an agreement with the New Jersey Attorney General to divest certain facilities within the state.
