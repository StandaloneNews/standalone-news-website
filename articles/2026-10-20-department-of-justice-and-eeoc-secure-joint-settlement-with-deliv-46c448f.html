Department of Justice and EEOC Secure Joint Settlement With Delivery Service

The Department of Justice and the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission announced they have secured a settlement with Maplebear Inc., doing business as Instacart, an online delivery service based in San Francisco, California.

The agencies investigated allegations that Instacart discriminated against U.S. and American workers by favoring temporary employment visa holders during the Permanent Labor Certification process.

Investigators found that Instacart imposed additional application burdens on U.S. workers by requiring physical mail submissions for PERM positions, differing from standard electronic application processes.

Under the terms of the agreement, Instacart is required to establish a compensation fund, pay civil penalties, accept electronic applications on its public career website, and train personnel on anti-discrimination obligations.
