Department of Justice Details Collaboration with State Attorneys General on Local Merger Reviews

The Department of Justice shared an update concerning the Antitrust Division's cooperative efforts with state Attorneys General in evaluating corporate mergers primarily affecting local jurisdictions. The update outlines how federal and state enforcers divide merger investigations based on regional and national interests.

The Department of Justice explained that federal enforcers generally focus their resources on regional or national transactions while occasionally reviewing local deals involving significant antitrust questions.

The Antitrust Division noted that state enforcers often take the lead on transactions affecting local interests, such as instances where a state acts as a direct purchaser or competition effects remain confined to one state.

The agency reported collaborating with state Attorneys General on various merger reviews, including joint investigations, consultations, and referrals that led to state-level settlements.
