A federal court in Arizona rejected a motion seeking to stop construction on the Hermosa Critical Minerals Project after finding that the plaintiffs failed to demonstrate a likelihood of success on their claims or likely irreparable harm.

The project is designed to create a domestic source of manganese and boost domestic zinc production, which support military equipment and advanced defense technology.

The U.S. Forest Service cleared the project following an environmental review spanning roughly three years and consultations under the Endangered Species Act, incorporating numerous binding conditions to reduce environmental effects.

Federal funding has been allocated to support the initiative, with awards provided under the Defense Production Act and through the Department of Energy.
