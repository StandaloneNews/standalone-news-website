The Federal Reserve published findings from the periodic survey detailing what households own, their borrowing levels, and banking habits, alongside a summary report examining shifts in income and net worth.

The agency reported that the homeownership rate remained stable, while the median net housing value for property-owning households increased.

The Federal Reserve noted that retirement plan participation increased slightly, and account balances rose for participating households.

The research organization NORC at the University of Chicago conducts the survey for the Board by randomly selecting participants from various geographic areas using scientific sampling.
