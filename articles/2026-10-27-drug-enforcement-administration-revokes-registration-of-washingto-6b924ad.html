Drug Enforcement Administration Revokes Registration of Washington Practitioner

The Drug Enforcement Administration announced the revocation of a Washington practitioner's registration following the suspension of her state medical license and her resulting lack of authority to handle controlled substances.

The Drug Enforcement Administration issued an Order to Show Cause proposing the revocation of the practitioner's registration because she lacked authority to handle controlled substances in the state where she was registered.

A state health department suspended the practitioner from practicing medicine after finding that she committed unprofessional conduct.

The Chief Administrative Law Judge granted the government's motion for summary disposition and recommended revoking the registration because the practitioner lacked state authority.

The agency adopted the administrative law judge's findings and ordered the revocation of the registration alongside the denial of any pending renewal applications.
