Federal Court Dismisses Three Challenges Against Gulf Energy Security Exemption

The Department of Justice announced that a federal court dismissed three separate lawsuits challenging a national security exemption for energy activities in the Gulf of America. The court ruled that these legal challenges must be heard in the courts of appeals rather than district courts.

The U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia dismissed three separate lawsuits brought against the Endangered Species Committee's national security exemption for oil and gas activities in the Gulf of America.

The presiding court determined that legal challenges regarding the exemption decisions must be reviewed exclusively in the federal courts of appeals rather than the district court.

The Endangered Species Committee previously voted unanimously to exempt Gulf of America oil and gas activities from specific requirements following a national security finding.
