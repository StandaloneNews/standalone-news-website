DEA Revokes Exempt Status for Inactive Butalbital Prescription Products

The Drug Enforcement Administration is revoking the exempted status for specific nonnarcotic prescription products listed on its Table of Exempted Prescription Products whose National Drug Codes are inactive because the items are no longer available or the originating company no longer exists.

The Drug Enforcement Administration is revoking the exempted status for certain nonnarcotic prescription products that currently appear on its designated table of exempted prescription products but feature inactive National Drug Codes.

The affected items will be removed from the agency table and will no longer maintain exempt prescription product status under the Controlled Substances Act.

The regulatory action does not have any impact on exempted prescription products that maintain active National Drug Code numbers.
