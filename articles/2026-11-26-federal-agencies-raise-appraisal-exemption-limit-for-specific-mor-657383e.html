Federal Agencies Raise Appraisal Exemption Limit for Specific Mortgage Loans

Federal regulators announced an upward adjustment to the loan size threshold exempting certain higher-priced mortgages from specific appraisal rules, following an annual review of consumer price index changes.

Federal regulatory bodies stated that the threshold amount determining which higher-priced mortgage loans are subject to specific appraisal rules will rise from $34,200 to $35,500.

The adjusted monetary limit was determined by calculating the annual percentage increase in a specific consumer price index recorded as of the middle of the year.

Statutory provisions mandate that creditors secure a written appraisal derived from an interior physical inspection prior to issuing certain higher-priced mortgage loans, though smaller loans qualify for an exemption.
