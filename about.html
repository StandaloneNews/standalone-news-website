OUR APPROACH INDEPENDENT NEWS COVERAGE

Real news.
Clearly sourced.

Standalone News is a digital-first headline and news-discovery project focused on major developments across the United States and worldwide.

01 / SOURCES

Transparency first

We collect public feed metadata from official agencies and news publishers and discover additional reporting through news aggregators. Each news item identifies its publisher and directs readers to the linked coverage. News discovery feeds are not the same as official government statements or direct licensed wire services.

02 / VERIFICATION

Speed is not certainty

A fast headline is not automatically a verified fact. Automatically collected headlines are attributed to their publishers and must not be mistaken for independent confirmation by Standalone News. Information may change during a developing story.

03 / GOVERNMENT

Read the primary document

For agency policy, regulation, enforcement and drug-scheduling developments, readers should consult the official document linked by the source. Proposals, notices, and final rules are distinct kinds of government action.

04 / RIGHTS

Original reporting stays with its publisher

Standalone News does not republish entire publisher articles in its public feed. The linked reporting remains with its original publisher, whose terms and access restrictions apply.

05 / CONTACT

Corrections and inquiries

For corrections, missing attribution or questions, contact us through our official X profile. We aim to distinguish confirmed facts from reporting that is preliminary or disputed.
