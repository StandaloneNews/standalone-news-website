OUR NEWSROOM / OUR STANDARDS

Trust begins
with the source.

Standalone News is a digital-first news-discovery service focused on major developments in the United States and around the world.

01 / SOURCES

Attribution is essential

Our public feed presents news metadata from official agencies and attributed publishers, as well as discovery links from news aggregation sources. Headlines identify the originating source and link readers to the associated publication. A discovery link is not the same as a direct official announcement.

02 / VERIFICATION

Speed is not certainty

Rapid news discovery does not independently verify every underlying report. Developing stories can change. We distinguish official announcements from third-party coverage and encourage readers to consult the original source for full reporting and updates.

03 / GOVERNMENT

Official records matter

For policy changes, agency enforcement, DEA scheduling, and other regulatory developments, original government documents are important. Proposed rules, notices, hearings, and final actions are not interchangeable; readers should review the linked primary record.

04 / ORIGINAL REPORTING

Credit the publishers

We do not reproduce complete articles from other publishers in the headline feed. Links direct readers to source websites, whose own terms and access conditions apply.

05 / CONTACT

Corrections and feedback

To flag a correction or missing attribution, contact us through our official X account (opens in a new tab). Please include the headline and original source where possible.

06 / AUTOMATION & AI

Automated explainers are identified

Some explainers are generated and published by an AI-assisted process without individual human review. These articles are visibly labeled, sourced from official announcements, and automatically screened for basic supporting evidence. Automated screening is not independent fact-checking and cannot guarantee accuracy. The system skips many sensitive or disputed stories; our news discovery feed continues to link to the original reporting. Corrections can be requested via our X account.
