STANDALONE NEWS — COMPLETE APP-STYLE REDESIGN
GitHub repo: https://github.com/StandaloneNews/standalone-news-website
Website: https://standalonenews.com

INSTALL — NO CODING REQUIRED

1. Make sure the existing Standalone News site and GitHub Actions publisher are functioning.
2. Download and extract Standalone_News_App_Style_Redesign.zip on your PC.
3. In GitHub, open the repository on branch "main".
4. Choose Add file > Upload files.
5. Drag ONLY THESE FOUR FILES into the ROOT of the repository:
     index.html
     news-app.css
     news-app.js
     styles.css
   All four files belong alongside your current "app.js" and "styles.css".
   Do not place them in the "assets", "data", "scripts", or ".github" folders.
6. GitHub may warn that index.html and styles.css already exist: they SHOULD be replaced.
7. Click Commit changes on branch main. Wait for Cloudflare to deploy, then refresh your site.
8. If the old page persists, try Ctrl+Shift+R or use an incognito window.

DO NOT replace or delete:
  .github/workflows/refresh-headlines.yml
  scripts/update_news.py
  scripts/auto_publish.py
  data/news.json
  data/articles.json
  articles/*.html
  feed.xml / sitemap.xml

What changes
- Entire new app-style homepage with desktop sidebar and mobile bottom tab navigation.
- Today, the Five-Story Brief, News, Markets, Explainers, and Saved views.
- Clear story cards, link-to-source buttons, search, topic filters, personal preferences.
- Save stories locally in the user's browser (no signup or server database).
- The root styles.css also gives bot-generated article pages and the legacy articles index
  a consistent visual treatment, without editing the working article publisher.

What stays the same
- Your current headline source feed and its update schedule.
- Gemini article generation, publishing frequency and its safety checks.
- Existing article URLs, article JSON format, official sources and RSS/sitemap generation.
- Cloudflare hosting and GoDaddy domain setup.

How the content works
- The homepage loads ./data/news.json and ./data/articles.json.
- Optional ./data/editorial.json is also supported if it exists.
- "Markets" means market-related headlines, NOT real-time stock quotes.
- The 5-Story Brief is an automated cross-topic selection, NOT an independently
  fact-checked editorial ranking. External reports open at their original publisher.
- AI articles are conspicuously labeled "AI-assisted / not human-reviewed".
- If the news feed or articles database contains no items, the app shows honest
  empty states rather than fake live stories.

PREVIEWS
The desktop, phone and article screenshots were made using SAMPLE HEADLINES.
Those screenshots are NOT live news content and are not needed for installation.
The app doesn't add any sample headlines to your live feeds.

ROLLBACK
If you want to revert, open GitHub commit history and revert the design commit.
That restores the old index.html and styles.css. The new news-app.css and news-app.js
can remain unused, or be deleted after the rollback.
