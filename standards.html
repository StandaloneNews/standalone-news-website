How Standalone News works.

A clear distinction between source-linked reporting, automated selection, and AI-assisted explainers.

Where headlines come from

Our automated desk collects public RSS feeds and news discovery results from publishers and official agencies. Headlines link to the original reporting or, when the discovery source is Google News, a coverage page. We do not claim that automated collection independently verifies every headline.

How stories are ranked

Software assigns priority using freshness, subject-matter signals, and source variety. Major economic releases and government actions can rank higher, but ranking does not mean editorial endorsement or fact-checking. Automated selections are labeled “Desk pick · automated.” Readers can browse the chronological news wire separately.

Why a story matters

Original AI-assisted explainers are presented separately from publisher headlines. When a related official-source explainer is available, the site may link to it for additional context. A headline alone is not sufficient evidence to produce a factual “why it matters” claim. Readers should consult the linked official documents and publishers for original facts.

Images and credits

Photos are selected as illustrations of identifiable institutions, locations or subjects, not presented as photographs of a breaking event unless independently established. The automated image matcher favors commercially reusable images from Wikimedia Commons and only uses pictures when subject relevance and license metadata meet its filters. Image credits and license links accompany photographs. When no suitable photo is available, Standalone News uses original category artwork instead. An image’s presence does not establish that it depicts the event in the headline.

Artificial intelligence

Automated headline ranking and image selection use rule-based methods; they do not require a paid language-model call for each visitor. Separately marked “AI-assisted” explainers may use Gemini and official documents. These explainers are not represented as human-reviewed unless that review has actually occurred. AI-generated content can make mistakes, so links to primary documents matter.

Markets and timestamps

Stock and fund price displays are provided by TradingView. Prices may be delayed and some instruments are ETFs rather than futures or official Treasury yields. Published news times appear in 24-hour UTC. This website does not give personalized investment advice.

Saved stories, follows, and email

Signed-in users can sync their reading list using Supabase. Topic and publisher following preferences currently save only in the browser on the device where they are selected, not automatically in the cloud. The optional email newsletter requires separate opt-in, email confirmation, and a verified email provider before activation. Links in emails allow readers to unsubscribe.

Corrections and transparency

If you notice a misleading attribution, incorrect illustration, or erroneous explanation, please contact Standalone News through its published X account and identify the specific headline or URL. Correction submissions are not guaranteed to be monitored in real time. We recommend checking the underlying publisher's updates for live corrections while contacting the publisher directly for errors in their reporting.
