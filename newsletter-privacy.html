Email newsletter privacy

Only if you subscribe: Standalone News collects your email address, whether you requested significant-story alerts, and timestamps recording your signup and confirmation. We use this information to send the source-linked daily news digest and alerts you choose.

How subscriptions work

You must explicitly opt in and confirm your address by email. When the newsletter backend is enabled, Supabase stores subscriber details and delivery records, and Resend processes addresses and messages for delivery. The supplied newsletter code uses the address to deliver opted-in messages; it does not implement advertising targeting. This is not an audit of provider practices.

Unsubscribing

Every digest and alert includes an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing stops future email deliveries and leaves a suppression record to avoid accidentally emailing you again. Contact Standalone News through its published X account for other account-data or privacy questions. No response time is guaranteed.

Reader accounts

Reader accounts and saved article sync are separate from email newsletter subscriptions. Following topics and publishers currently stays in your browser, unless you explicitly subscribe to the email list.
