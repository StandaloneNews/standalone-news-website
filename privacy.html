Privacy Policy

How reader accounts, saved stories, browser preferences and optional newsletter data are handled.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Information we process

You can read public headlines without creating an account. When you create a reader account, our authentication provider, Supabase, processes your email address and login information. When you save articles while signed in, the service stores the article links and related metadata so your reading list can sync between devices.

Topic preferences, followed publishers, and some saved links are stored locally in your browser. Browser storage is used to remember those choices; clearing it may remove local preferences. We may receive ordinary web-server request logs, including IP addresses, browser information and request times, through our hosting infrastructure.

If you choose to subscribe to the email briefing, the separate newsletter system collects your email address, opt-in choices, confirmation time, and delivery or unsubscribe status. It uses Supabase for subscriber records and Resend for email delivery when those services are configured. Newsletter enrollment requires confirmation. Reader accounts and newsletter subscriptions are separate.

How information is used

  • Deliver public news pages, content links, and market widgets.
  • Authenticate optional reader accounts and synchronize saved article links.
  • Remember browser-local preferences and display relevant topics.
  • Send opted-in emails and maintain suppression records to respect unsubscribes, when newsletter delivery is enabled.
  • Diagnose reliability and security problems and respond to lawful requests.

Outside services and outbound links

Our website uses or may use Supabase (authentication and saved links), Cloudflare (hosting and security), TradingView (embedded market data), Wikimedia Commons and licensed image sources, and Resend (opt-in newsletter delivery). Those providers have their own privacy practices. Clicking a linked publisher headline opens an outside site governed by that publisher's terms and privacy practices.

Third-party market widgets may process technical identifiers or set storage under their own policies. Review your browser settings if you wish to limit optional site storage or external embeds.

Retention and your choices

You can remove saved links and change topic or publisher follows in the website interface. You can unsubscribe from newsletter emails using the link included with each message; suppression records may remain to help prevent future sends. Account and newsletter records may also be kept as needed for operations, security and legal requirements. Specific provider retention periods can differ.

For questions about access, correction, or deletion of personal information, contact us using the postal address below or the contact methods on our Contact & corrections page. Please don't post private account details publicly. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy law.

Security, children and changes

We use hosted service providers and access controls intended to protect account and newsletter data, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. The site is a general-audience news service and is not intentionally designed to collect personal data from children under 13. We may update this notice when site features or providers change; updates will be reflected by the date above.
