Privacy Policy

What this website stores, what it does not ask for, and what other services may process.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

What Standalone News stores

  • Without an account: You can read public news without signing in. If you save stories as a guest, the site stores saved links, titles, publisher names, categories, dates and available descriptions in your browser's local storage.
  • Reader accounts (optional): Supabase processes your email address and passwordless sign-in session. If you save stories while signed in, the saved links and their associated metadata are stored in Supabase so they can sync across devices.
  • Community discussions (if you participate): The site stores the public username you choose, chat messages, discussion posts, replies, timestamps, room/topic selection, and your account ID in Supabase. Public posts and usernames are visible to visitors. Private reports, their reasons and details, and moderation restrictions are stored for review; moderators can see them. The site does not display your sign-in email alongside community posts.
  • Reading preferences: Followed publishers and topic choices are stored in this browser's local storage. The current implementation does not sync those preferences with your account.
  • Email newsletter (only if enabled and you opt in): The newsletter system stores your email address, whether you requested optional alerts, consent and confirmation timestamps, confirmation-token hashes, unsubscribe status and delivery records in Supabase. Resend processes addresses and messages to deliver emails. Newsletter subscriptions are separate from reader accounts.

What the current website does not ask you to provide

  • No credit-card numbers, banking details or payment information for reading news, saving stories or subscribing to the newsletter.
  • No account password in the website's passwordless email sign-in form.
  • No precise GPS location, government identity document or contact list for these features.
  • No newsletter signup from simply reading a headline; subscription requires you to enter an email address and confirm it.

These are statements about the supplied Standalone News forms and application code. They do not mean that our hosting provider or third-party content providers collect no technical data.

Tracking, storage and your choices

The reviewed website scripts use browser local storage for guest bookmarks, followed publishers and topic preferences. The supplied homepage code does not implement an advertising tracker or a separate visitor-behavior analytics library. This is not a guarantee that Cloudflare, embedded widgets, browser extensions or other third parties do not log requests or use identifiers.

You can clear browser-local data using your browser settings. Signed-in readers can remove saved stories in the site interface. Community content is public to readers until moderated or removed; moderators can hide reported content and restrict posting. For removal of your community data, contact us; do not assume deleting your account immediately erases published content or any provider logs. Newsletter subscribers can unsubscribe through the link in each email; a suppression or delivery record may be retained to respect that choice. For other access or deletion questions, see Contact & corrections.

We do not have confirmed retention periods for every provider or infrastructure log, so we do not promise that all data is deleted immediately or automatically after a fixed number of days.

Data use, security and updates

In the reviewed website implementation, reader account information is used for authentication, saved-story records for synchronization, preferences for on-device personalization, and newsletter records for opt-in email delivery. The supplied code does not implement selling newsletter email addresses, saved-article histories, or community messages to advertisers. This does not constitute an independent audit of every connected provider or administrative practice.

Security measures include Supabase access controls and server-side secrets for the optional newsletter backend, but no online system can guarantee absolute security. This policy may be revised when features or providers change.
