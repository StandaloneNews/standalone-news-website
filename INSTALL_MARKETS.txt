STANDALONE NEWS — MARKETS + BONDS UPDATE (24-hour UTC format preserved)

WHAT THIS DOES
- Adds an always-visible scrolling markets ticker.
- Replaces the Markets section with: major U.S. indexes, popular stocks, index futures, oil, gold, silver, U.S. Treasury yields, Treasury futures PRICES, and bond ETFs.
- Adds a Markets tab in mobile navigation.
- Preserves headline RSS, generated AI explainers, saved stories, and dark mode.
- Market quotes are provided by embedded TradingView widgets. They can be delayed or unavailable because of exchange restrictions, third-party blocking, or market hours. No market price is fabricated by this site.
- No API key required in the public website.

INSTALLATION
1. BACK UP your existing repository files if you have since customized them.
2. Upload index.html and news-app.css into the ROOT of StandaloneNews/standalone-news-website on GitHub (same level as news-app.js). Replace the existing versions.
3. Do not upload INSTALL_MARKETS.txt if you don't want to.
4. Leave news-app.js and styles.css in place: this upgrade is designed for the app redesign package. If you have not installed the 24-hour app redesign, upload news-app.js and styles.css from this ZIP too.
5. Commit to main and allow Cloudflare to deploy. Test on desktop and mobile, and disable ad/privacy blockers temporarily if the market widgets do not load.

PLEASE NOTE
The market widgets require browser access to https://widgets.tradingview-widget.com/ . If you enforce a strict Content Security Policy, add the third-party widget host to the relevant script/frame/connect directives per TradingView documentation.
Instruments like CBOT:ZN1! are TREASURY FUTURES contract prices, not individual cash-bond prices. The 2Y/5Y/10Y/30Y instruments TVC:US..Y are YIELDS, expressed as percentages.
The website uses UTC 24-hour time for news feed timestamps.
TradingView attribution remains visible and links to its site.

The U.S. 10-year minus 2-year yield spread is linked to its official FRED series: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/T10Y2Y (daily data, not an intraday quote).
